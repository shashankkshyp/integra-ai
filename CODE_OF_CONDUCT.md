# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in the Integra.AI project and community, regardless of experience level, background, identity, appearance, or personal circumstances.

We want our community to be a place where people can ask questions, share ideas, learn, collaborate, and contribute constructively.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, project-related events, and other spaces where someone is representing the Integra.AI project.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Critiquing ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Assuming good intent while remaining open to the impact of our words and actions.
- Respecting different viewpoints, communication styles, and levels of experience.
- Keeping discussions relevant, professional, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or deliberate personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, derogatory, or demeaning comments.
- Sexual language, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualised imagery in project spaces.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Doxxing, stalking, or encouraging others to target a person.
- Insults or hostile conduct in code reviews, issues, pull requests, or direct project communication.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make a person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

Disagreement about technical decisions is normal. It must remain respectful and must not become personal.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate it. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Maintainers should communicate moderation decisions clearly and apply this policy fairly and consistently.

## Reporting unacceptable behaviour

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

- **Preferred route:** use a private contact method listed on the project maintainer's GitHub profile: [@srnsksyp](https://github.com/srnsksyp).
- **Do not** report sensitive conduct concerns in a public issue, pull request, or discussion.
- Include relevant context, links, dates, and screenshots where safe and appropriate. Avoid sharing unrelated personal information.

If you cannot find a suitable private contact method, use GitHub's available private reporting or contact options. Please do not create a public issue containing sensitive details.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Information will be shared only with people who need it to assess and respond to the report. The project cannot guarantee a particular response time or outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate to stop unacceptable behaviour and protect the community. Depending on the severity and context, responses may include:

1. A private or public warning.
2. A request to stop the behaviour or edit/remove inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions from project spaces.
4. Removal of comments, contributions, or access.
5. Permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is not acceptable and may itself result in enforcement action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing Integra.AI in public spaces. Representation includes using an official project account or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
